Connect WordPress forms to your Dash Dolphin account. Get SMS and email alerts on every form submission, with smart inquiry summaries and smart filtering.

== Description ==

Stop missing leads. Dash Dolphin sends you an SMS or email the moment a visitor submits any form on your site, so you can follow up before your competitors do. Every alert includes a smart inquiry summary that pulls out the key details, and smart filtering keeps noise out of your inbox by routing only the submissions you care about.

Dash Dolphin connects directly to your existing forms, with no changes to your form setup or styling. Supported form plugins: Gravity Forms, WPForms, Contact Form 7, Elementor Forms, Fluent Forms, Forminator, Ninja Forms, and Formidable Forms.

A Dash Dolphin account is required. Sign up at https://dashdolphin.com.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `dash-dolphin` folder to your `/wp-content/plugins/` directory, or install it directly from your WordPress admin via Plugins > Add New.
2. Activate the plugin through the Plugins menu in your WordPress admin.
3. Go to Settings > Dash Dolphin in your WordPress admin.
4. Enter your API key. You can find or create your API key at https://app.dashdolphin.com/settings/api-keys.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Do I need a Dash Dolphin account? =

Yes. A Dash Dolphin account is required to use this plugin. You can sign up for free at https://dashdolphin.com. The plugin will not send any alerts until you connect your API key in Settings > Dash Dolphin.

= Which form plugins are supported? =

Dash Dolphin works with the following WordPress form plugins: Gravity Forms, WPForms, Contact Form 7, Elementor Forms, Fluent Forms, Forminator, Ninja Forms, and Formidable Forms. If you use a different form plugin, please contact us at https://dashdolphin.com and we will evaluate adding support.

= Will this modify my forms? =

No. Dash Dolphin does not modify your forms, their fields, or their styling in any way. It listens for submission events and forwards relevant data to your Dash Dolphin account. Your forms continue to work exactly as before.

== Privacy ==

This plugin only transmits data to Dash Dolphin servers when you have explicitly connected your API key in the plugin settings. Data sent includes form field values from submissions on your site. No data is collected or transmitted before the API key is configured. For full details, see the Dash Dolphin privacy policy at https://dashdolphin.com/privacy.
